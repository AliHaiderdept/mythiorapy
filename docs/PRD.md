# Meridian — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Product summary
Meridian is a privacy-conscious mental wellness companion that helps people notice patterns, complete self-reflection assessments, review progress, and access calming educational resources. It supports wellbeing and does not diagnose or replace professional care.

## 2. Goals
- Make daily emotional check-ins quick and non-judgmental.
- Turn assessment and quiz results into clear, actionable reflections.
- Help users observe trends across mood, sleep, focus, calm, and habits.
- Offer a trustworthy library of books, guided sessions, expert stories, and personalized plans.
- Present premium value without obstructing essential safety and wellbeing features.

## 3. Target users
- Adults building a daily self-reflection habit.
- People managing everyday stress or anxiety with professional support.
- Users who value private, calm, evidence-informed digital experiences.

## 4. Core experience
1. User opens the dashboard and sees today's stability summary.
2. User logs a mood or starts a breathing session.
3. User completes a short assessment or quiz.
4. Results appear in a detail card with score, contributing areas, and guidance.
5. History and analytics reveal trends over time.
6. Reports can be reviewed or shared at the user's discretion.
7. The content library suggests relevant books, sessions, and expert information.

## 5. Functional requirements
### Dashboard
- Stability index, trend delta, streak, average mood, and session count.
- Daily check-in and breathing shortcuts.
- Current assessment progress and latest result summary.
- Premium content recommendations.

### Assessments and quizzes
- Multi-step questions, progress indicator, save/resume, submission, and result summary.
- Results must use supportive language and avoid diagnostic claims.
- Show timestamp, score band, category breakdown, and suggested next steps.

### History and reports
- Chronological check-ins, assessments, quiz results, and completed activities.
- Filter by date and activity type.
- Printable/exportable reports in a future release.

### Information, books, and content
- Searchable educational content organized by format and topic.
- Reading/session duration, author or expert attribution, and completion state.
- Premium items are clearly labeled.

### Analytics
- Weekly and monthly trends.
- Correlations should be framed as observations, not medical conclusions.

### Settings
- Profile, privacy, reminders, accessibility, data export, and account deletion.

## 6. Premium offering
- Personalized weekly plans.
- Full guided audio library.
- Expert-led courses and stories.
- Advanced trend comparisons and extended reports.
- Offline saved content in a future release.

Essential crisis information, account privacy controls, and baseline check-ins must never be paywalled.

## 7. Landing-page content options
- Interactive guided-breathing preview.
- Anonymous recovery stories with consent.
- Evidence and methodology library.
- Personalized welcome-path selector.
- Weekly expert session spotlight.
- Privacy pledge with plain-language controls.
- A “What Meridian is / is not” trust section.

## 8. Success metrics
- Daily check-in completion rate.
- Seven-day retention and streak continuation.
- Assessment completion rate.
- Helpful-content saves and completions.
- Report opens after assessment completion.
- Premium trial conversion without reduced access to safety resources.

## 9. Safety and compliance
- Prominent non-diagnostic disclaimer and localized crisis direction.
- Minimize sensitive data and make deletion/export understandable.
- Obtain explicit consent for reminders and analytics.
- Clinical content requires qualified review before release.
