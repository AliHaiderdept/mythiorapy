# Meridian — Product Rules

## Safety
1. Meridian is a wellbeing tool, not a diagnostic or emergency service.
2. Crisis directions remain visible, localized, and free.
3. Never infer a diagnosis from a score or trend.
4. Concerning answers may present supportive resources but must not use alarmist language.
5. Clinical content requires qualified human review.

## Privacy
1. Collect only data needed for the stated experience.
2. Ask consent before reminders, personalization, or optional analytics.
3. Users can export and delete their data.
4. Sensitive journal or assessment text is excluded from marketing analytics.
5. Privacy defaults to the most protective reasonable setting.

## Experience
1. Essential check-ins and safety information cannot be paywalled.
2. Every score includes context and a plain-language explanation.
3. The interface must support keyboard use and reduced motion.
4. No dark patterns, artificial urgency, or shame-based streak messaging.
5. Premium labeling must be clear before an action begins.

## Content
1. Cite the reviewer and review date for evidence-informed material.
2. Use inclusive language and diverse imagery.
3. Avoid promises of cure, guaranteed improvement, or professional substitution.
4. Review crisis resources and external links on a scheduled basis.
